## What this slice is

Migration 0017 `market_daily_summary` (immutable by trigger, `price_basis` CHECK 'asking', primary key `(location_id, dataset, summary_date, series, cohort_key, calc_version)`), `rm_core.market_history` (pure `build_rows`, `freshness`, `PgMarketHistory` publisher and read adapter), `deal_analyzer.market_stats.summarise_asking` (the only place quantiles are computed; `rm_core` receives it as an injected callable, because `packages/*` do not depend on `apps/*`). Two series that never mix: `additions` (offers whose source date is the day, plus a separate `first_observed_count`) and `cohort` (rolling 90-elapsed-day eligible cohort at `as_of`). Cohort keys: `all`, `rooms_1..3`, `rooms_4plus`, size bands. Publication is atomic per location-day under an advisory lock, once per `CALC_VERSION`. Only offers from `published` runs count. Past days are backfilled with `as_of` at the end of that day and refused before the bootstrap date. Reader returns `AskingRentObservation` (price_basis literal `asking`, caveats) and `MarketView` (live freshness from the checkpoint, `usable_for_ranking`). No API route. Batch task `publish_market_summaries` (periodic `30 5 * * *`, queue `batch`, gated by `OTODOM_MARKET_IMPORT_ENABLED`).
