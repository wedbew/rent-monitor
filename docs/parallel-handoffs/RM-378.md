## Blockers, limitations and decisions

- Policy deliberately left open (B-023, do not ask again for GUS reading or scope, only these): metric selection and reviewed
  `bdl_variable_id`s, `PublicationPolicy` thresholds, scoring weights (LCTX-02, not started), source max age, fiscal comparability.
  The catalogue of the 24 reference candidates is not encoded: no metric is invented as selected.
- Conventions I chose and the owner may change (versioned by `RANKING_METHOD_VERSION`): rank 1 is the highest value unless
  `lower_is_favorable`; percentile is `(behind + 0.5 * equal incl. self) / n`, 4 decimals, half-even; percent change only for
  `percent`-basis metrics with a positive base.
- Dependency note: `rm_core.location_contracts` imports `rm_contracts` but `packages/rm_core/pyproject.toml` does not declare
  `rm-contracts`. Works in the workspace (both installed); I did not touch manifests or `uv.lock` (reserved). Integrator decision:
  declare it, or move the logic module into `rm_contracts`.
- Legal / GDPR: tags `LEGAL[third-party-data]` and `GDPR[processor]` (no personal data) in both module docstrings; `LegalTag` enum unchanged.
