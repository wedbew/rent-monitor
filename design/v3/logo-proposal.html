CONCEPT 09 — ELECTROLIZE / BOLD

Straight lines. Clear identity.

A bold capital “R” in Electrolize, centred inside a solid square. The letter is white; the square uses the same ink-green colour as the renti wordmark.

Stronger letter weight and a solid background give the symbol more presence at small sizes. Corners stay sharp. The dark-surface version reverses the colours to preserve contrast.
