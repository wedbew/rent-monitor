# Renti identity

13 October 2026 · Revised wordmark for the V3 prototype; domain: renti.tech.

One name, one mark: **renti**. The detached initial has been removed. Custom filled lowercase letterforms use a compact rhythm, strong verticals and open counters. The split rectangular dot above the i suggests a pair of lit windows. This small architectural signature belongs inside the word rather than becoming a second symbol beside it.

The master is entirely vector paths, with no font dependency. Forest green is used on light surfaces, pale mineral white on dark surfaces. Headers on all five pages share the same paths and accessible name.

- `renti-logo.svg`: transparent forest-green wordmark.
- `renti-logo-dark.svg`: transparent pale wordmark.
- `renti-icon.svg`: compact lowercase r for browser/favicon use only; never placed before the full wordmark.

Use the original proportions and clear space of one stem width. Application widths are 100–112 px. The domain suffix is not part of the logo. Historical V1/V2 designs are unchanged.
